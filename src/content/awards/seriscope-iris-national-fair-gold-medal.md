SeriScope won a Gold Medal in Computational Biology and Bioinformatics at the IRIS National Fair, held from 29 to 31 January 2026 at the Dr. Ambedkar International Centre, New Delhi. The fair was organised by Exstemplar Education Linkers Foundation, powered by Zydus Lifesciences Limited, and supported by the Zydus Foundation and the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India.
